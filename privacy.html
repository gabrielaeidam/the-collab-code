Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Collab Code ("we," "us," or "our") collects and uses information when you visit thecollabcode.co or purchase The Collab Code.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you purchase or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, billing details, and anything you include in your messages.

Payment information. Payments are processed by our third-party checkout provider. We do not see or store your full card number.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our site and service providers may collect basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you visit.

How we use your information

  • To process your purchase and give you access to the program.
  • To send important messages about your purchase, access, or updates to the program.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To send marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.
  • To keep our website secure and improve it.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Service providers

We use trusted third parties to run The Collab Code, such as our website host, our checkout and payment provider, our course platform, and our email provider. Our website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them. These providers only receive the information they need to do their job and handle it under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

Our website itself does not use advertising cookies. Our checkout and course platforms may use cookies to keep you logged in, process payments, and keep things secure. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as you have access to the program and as needed for legal, tax, and accounting purposes. After that, we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you marketing emails, by emailing gaby@thecollabcode.co. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor those requests as required.

Children

The Collab Code is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at gaby@thecollabcode.co.
